# Intelligence Architecture

Economic Intelligence OS is designed as an orchestration system, not a single-model wrapper.

## Intelligence layers

1. **Intent & context** — understand the user's objective, organization and constraints.
2. **Company Brain** — retrieve authorized organizational knowledge and prior decisions.
3. **Research** — collect current external evidence with provenance and freshness metadata.
4. **AI Board** — independent specialist deliberation across strategy, risk, finance, operations and criticism.
5. **Verification** — validate structure, evidence, contradictions, confidence and safety.
6. **Decision** — synthesize options while preserving dissent and uncertainty.
7. **Execution** — convert approved decisions into controlled actions and workflows.
8. **Outcome learning** — measure realized results and feed them back into future decisions.

## Non-negotiable design rules

- No single AI provider is a system-wide dependency.
- High-impact claims require evidence/provenance when evidence is available.
- Uncertainty and disagreement must remain visible.
- Organization data is isolated by tenant and authorization context.
- High-risk content passes through dedicated safety controls.
- External actions require explicit authorization policies and auditability.
- Model cost, latency and reliability are measured per task.
- Sensitive prompts, outputs and secrets must never be exposed through logs by default.

## Competitive objective

Power is measured by decision quality, reliability, breadth of execution, learning speed and economic value created — not by raw model size or unsupported claims of being the world's most powerful AI.
